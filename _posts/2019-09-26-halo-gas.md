By analyzing the signal from a localized fast radio burst that passed through the
halo of a massive foreground galaxy, our team characterized the diffuse gas and
magnetic field of that halo &mdash; demonstrating a new and transformative
technique for probing the gas around galaxies. The work appeared in *Science*.

*Featured in [UC Santa Cruz News](https://news.ucsc.edu/2019/09/fast-radio-burst/).*
