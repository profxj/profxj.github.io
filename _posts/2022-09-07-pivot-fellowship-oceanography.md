A [Simons Foundation Pivot Fellowship](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/simons-pivot-fellowship/)
is supporting my move into **oceanography**, applying the data-science and
spectroscopy methods I developed in astronomy to problems in physical and
biological oceanography &mdash; including a fellowship year at the Scripps
Institution of Oceanography.

*Featured in [UC Santa Cruz News](https://news.ucsc.edu/2022/09/prochaska-pivot-fellow/).*
