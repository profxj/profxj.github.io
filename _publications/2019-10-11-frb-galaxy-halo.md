The sightline to the fast radio burst FRB 181112 passes through the halo of a
foreground massive galaxy. The small scattering and rotation measure imprinted
on the burst place tight upper limits on the density, turbulence, and magnetic
field of the circumgalactic medium, revealing a surprisingly quiescent halo.
