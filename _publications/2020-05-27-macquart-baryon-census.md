Roughly half of the ordinary (baryonic) matter predicted by Big Bang
nucleosynthesis had eluded direct detection in the local Universe. This paper
uses a sample of localized fast radio bursts &mdash; whose dispersion measures
record the free electrons encountered along each sightline &mdash; to perform a
direct census of these diffuse baryons, establishing the dispersion
measure&ndash;redshift ("Macquart") relation and recovering the cosmic baryon
density in agreement with the Cosmic Microwave Background.
