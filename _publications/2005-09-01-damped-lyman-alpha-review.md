Damped Ly&alpha; systems (DLAs) are the highest-column-density absorbers seen in
quasar spectra and contain the bulk of the neutral hydrogen available for star
formation at high redshift. This review surveys their incidence, kinematics,
chemical abundances, and connection to galaxy formation, synthesizing two
decades of observational and theoretical work.
